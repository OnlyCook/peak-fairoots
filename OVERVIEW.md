## Why:
Roots is only the second biome and is (subjectively) the least balanced: on some seeds you can beat it without ever touching anything but tree leaves and on others you question the energy used to play this game. Although often not because of it being overly challenging but instead because of how unfair and frustrating it feels. I think this is caused by the fact that there are too little mechanics to address the many new hazards. This mod tries to fix exactly that while also making it more tolerable to traverse this biome in general.
